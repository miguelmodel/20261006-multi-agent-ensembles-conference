How long can an agent work on its own?

Length of software tasks (timed by human experts) that AI agents complete successfully 50% of the time.

Source: METR, "Task-Completion Time Horizons of Frontier AI Models" (Time Horizon 1.1), metr.org/time-horizons, last updated May 8, 2026.

One sentence does the work: "Capability is no longer the bottleneck. Managing it is." Then go straight to slide 8.

If asked what the chart measures: METR's time horizon is the length of software tasks, timed by human experts, that AI agents complete successfully 50% of the time. It is not how long an agent runs on its own; METR calls it a measure of task difficulty. The solid line ends at Claude Opus 4.6, about 11 h 59 min, which is the latest point METR treats as reliable. METR says measurements above 16 hours are unreliable with its current task suite. That is why Claude Mythos Preview (early) appears only as a hollow "16 h+" marker; METR's own chart says "Likely at least 16 hrs". The doubling figure is METR's 128.744 days for models released from 2023 onward; "about 4 months" is our conversion. METR does not measure every model, so some recent models are not on the chart yet.
