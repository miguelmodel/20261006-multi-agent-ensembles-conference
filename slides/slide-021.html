Four layers of control

Agents reviewing each other

"Correct? Accurate? Up to date?"

You review a near-finished product, not a first draft.

Programmatic evaluations

Scripts check every output: format, metadata, titles, required inputs and outputs.

Cheap, so they run every time.

Agent-defined permissions & scopes

Each agent has its own permissions, MCPs and authentication.

Least privilege: you know who can touch what.

Human review gates

At the key points the workflow stops and waits for you to validate, verify and give feedback.
