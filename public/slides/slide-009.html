The challenges of a single agent

Accuracy

Hallucinations and mistakes.

One agent, nobody checking its work.

Context window

Even the largest windows (around 1M tokens) fill fast on a real project.

Long tasks, many files, many tools, and the model starts losing track.

Efficiency

One agent does one thing at a time.

No parallelism; every step waits on the last.

Security

One agent with every permission is one big blast radius.

If it is compromised or goes wrong, everything it can reach is at risk.

Say "blast radius" out loud on the security column; it is the phrase business people remember. Compare it to a single admin account that holds every key. Tell the audience these four lenses come back on the next slide and through the rest of the talk.
