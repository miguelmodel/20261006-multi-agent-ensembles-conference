Don't: one agent, every instruction, every tool.

One mega-agent

Polluted context, too many tools, worse results.

Four specialists

One job, one toolset, one context each.

Piling the whole workflow's instructions and every MCP onto one model instance pollutes its context and overwhelms it with too many tools and rules at once. Results get worse, not better. Specialization is the fix. Kept as its own slide (approved Oct 5); the Demo 2 script sits in this slide's speaker notes.

Pacing: about half a minute to a minute on the slide, then 3 to 4 minutes off slide for Demo 2.
